# Ingoude

**Ingoude Company** presents a business proposal for an online ingredient and meal-kit service designed to simplify home cooking.

## **1\. Core Concept**

The service provides pre-measured, dish-specific ingredient packages ("Cooking Kits") ordered online, accompanied by instructional video content to guide customers step-by-step through cooking.

## **2\. Key Problems Addressed**

The presentation highlights common pain points in home meal preparation:

* **Shopping difficulty**: Confusion over what ingredients to buy, required quantities, and time wasted going to the market.
* **Food waste**: Buying ingredients in excess leads to unnecessary waste and spending.
* **Cooking barriers**: Inexperience with cooking techniques or specific recipe steps.
* **Inconvenience**: Social media cooking videos demonstrate recipes, but viewers must still source and buy all ingredients themselves.

## **3\. Solution &amp; Benefits**

Ingoude Company provides an end-to-end solution:

* **Pre-portioned kits**: Delivers exact ingredient portions per recipe to prevent waste and control budgets.
* **Interactive media**: Leverages Live streams and short videos covering preparation, step-by-step cooking, portioning, and cooking tips.
* **Time savings**: Quick online ordering (taking \~20 minutes) with home delivery[4].
* **Fresh local produce**: Uses natural, locally sourced fresh products.

## **4\. Target Audience**

* **Students and working professionals** (ages 20–40) living alone or renting who want to cook for themselves.
* **Busy individuals and stay-at-home parents** who have limited time to visit fresh markets or live far from shopping centers.

## **5\. Operational Plan &amp; Execution**

* **Initial Launch**: Starting operations in **Battambang city**, prioritizing households near main delivery zones.
* **Package Customization**: Kits divided by serving size (e.g., 2–3 people or 4–5 people) with labeled dish names, ingredient lists, and pricing.
* **Sales &amp; Media Channels**: Promoting via Facebook, TikTok, and Telegram; accepting orders via social media or phone.
* **6-Month Vision**: Conduct local market research, form partnerships with delivery services, continue live content, and build a steady daily customer base[4].

## **6\. Competitive Edge**

Compared to traditional markets, Ingoude Company integrates online ordering, home delivery, pre-measured meal packages, step-by-step recipes, live cooking tutorials, and customized family portions into a single service[4].
